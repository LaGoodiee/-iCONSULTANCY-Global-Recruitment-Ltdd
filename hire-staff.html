About us

ICONSULTANCY GLOBAL RECRUITMENT LTD

ICONSULTANCY GLOBAL RECRUITMENT LTD was established on 5th October 2021 by Mallam Abdulkadir Muhammed. The company was formerly known as iCONSULTANCY 2021 before its evolution into a registered limited liability company.

We are a professional service organisation committed to providing reliable, innovative and result-oriented solutions in Recruitment, Staff Outsourcing, Human Resource Consultancy and Educational Services.

Our establishment is driven by the vision of contributing to national development by bridging the gap between employers and job seekers, improving workforce productivity and expanding access to quality educational services.

Our Vision

To become a leading and trusted provider of employment and educational services in Nigeria and beyond.

🚩

Our Mission

To reduce unemployment, promote educational development and provide reliable employment and educational solutions that create opportunities for individuals and organisations.

Our Core Objectives

1

Employment Creation

To contribute to reducing unemployment and associated social vices in Nigeria by creating access to meaningful employment opportunities and connecting qualified job seekers with prospective employers.

2

Staffing Solutions and Retention

To provide effective recruitment, workforce outsourcing, staffing and employee retention solutions that enable businesses and organisations to attract, manage and retain competent personnel.

3

Human Resource Consultancy

To offer comprehensive HR consultancy services, including workforce planning, employee management, HR advisory, recruitment support and organisational development.

4

Educational Services

To provide accessible, reliable and quality educational services that promote learning, academic excellence, professional development and lifelong learning.

Our Commitment

At ICONSULTANCY GLOBAL RECRUITMENT LTD, we believe that people are the greatest assets of every successful organisation. We are committed to empowering individuals, strengthening businesses and contributing to a more productive society through professional service delivery, integrity, innovation and excellence.
